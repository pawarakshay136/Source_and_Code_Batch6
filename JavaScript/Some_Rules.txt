====================================================
RULES BEFORE STARTING JAVASCRIPT
====================================================

Learning technical skills
requires DAILY PRACTICE and CONSISTENCY.

----------------------------------------------------
JAVASCRIPT
----------------------------------------------------

Every day:
- Attend a 45-minute to 1-hour teaching session.
- You will get 2–3 assignments.
- Assignments are based on concepts.
- Assignments are mainly based on logical thinking.
- Learning is also interview-oriented.

Support Session:
- Ask questions about assignments.
- Discuss concepts.
- Ask for more examples.
- Practice interview questions.
- Ask topic-related doubts.


WHAT HAPPENS IF YOU DON'T PRACTICE JAVASCRIPT?

1 Day:
- You may not feel much difference.

1 Week:
- You may start losing clarity.
- You may struggle with loops, strings, and other concepts.

1 Month:
- You may lose connection with the concepts.
- Your logical thinking may start getting weaker.
- You may fall behind the current market requirements.

1 Year:
- You may feel like you are back to nowhere.

IMPORTANT:
Logical thinking takes years to build.
It may take approximately 3–4 years of consistent
practice and experience to build strong logical skills.

We don't have unlimited time, so consistency is important.


====================================================
MANUAL TESTING
====================================================

1 Day:
- Relax / No major impact.

1 Week:
- You may start forgetting domain knowledge.
- Application knowledge may become weaker.

1 Month:
- Your ability to identify bugs effectively may reduce.

1 Year:
- You may go out of scope / lose connection
  with the testing field.

IMPORTANT:
Manual Testing requires approximately
2–3 months of consistent practice to build a foundation.


====================================================
GIT & GITHUB
====================================================

1 Day:
- You may forget some command syntax.

1 Week:
- You may start getting confused.
- Pull Requests (PRs) and workflows may become confusing.
- You may start questioning your own ability.

1 Month:
- You may remember only basic commands like:
  git add
  git commit

1 Year:
- You may completely lose connection
  with Git concepts and workflows.

IMPORTANT:
Git and GitHub may require approximately
4–5 months of regular practice to become comfortable.


====================================================
MAIN RULE:
====================================================

Learning → Practice → Forget → Revise → Practice Again

Consistency is more important than learning
a lot in one day and then stopping.

Don't just attend sessions.
Practice concepts and assignments regularly.
